THE CAPACITY CHALLENGE

Operating in the dark costs lives and resources.

Without accurate demand forecasting, hospital administrators are forced to be reactive, leading to bottlenecks and strained clinical teams.

🛏️

Bed Shortages

Unexpected patient surges lead to ED boarding, cancelled elective surgeries, and compromised patient care due to lack of space.

👩‍⚕️

Staffing Burnout

Over-scheduling wastes the hospital budget, while under-scheduling causes severe nurse burnout and violations of mandated ratios.

⏳

Reactive Operations

Relying on yesterday's data to plan tomorrow's shifts leaves hospital leadership constantly fighting fires instead of planning ahead.
